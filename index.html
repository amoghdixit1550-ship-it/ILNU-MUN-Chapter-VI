01

What is Model UN?

“At Model UN, you broaden your horizons. By learning and networking, you can be part of the UN's efforts to establish peace, secure human rights and enable all people to live in dignity.”

— António Guterres, United Nations Secretary-General

Model United Nations is an academic simulation of the United Nations where students play the role of delegates from different countries and attempt to solve real-world issues with the policies and perspectives of their assigned country. The conference process develops skills in international debate, negotiation and diplomacy.

02

About ILNUMUN

ILNU MUN, aligning with its global perspective mission, cultivates intellectual discourse through Model United Nations Conferences. The ILNU MUN Society, a collaborative effort between students and faculty, orchestrates immersive experiences and serves as a training ground for students tackling global issues.

Prior ILNU MUN successes have set a benchmark, earning local acclaim and enhancing the institution's reputation. Its impact, with hundreds of participants and collaborations with UN entities, extends through hospitality, rewards and inclusive events.

The VI Edition extends an open invitation to students to immerse themselves in the experience, contribute to the legacy, push boundaries and establish new benchmarks for excellence.

03

A LETTER FROM THE COMMITTEE

To the Young Minds

It is the distinct honor of the ILNUMUN Committee to welcome delegates to the sixth edition of the Institute of Law, Nirma University Model United Nations Conference. ILNU stands as an institution committed to academic rigor, interdisciplinary learning and justice education, while ILNUMUN extends that vision into an immersive arena for youth leadership, diplomatic discourse and global advocacy.
